LiqhtApp

Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026 · applies to LiqhtApp for Android (app.light.wallet)

The short version

LiqhtApp itself collects nothing for analytics: no accounts, no tracking, no crash reporting and no advertising.

Your Lightning node runs on an ember server, and LiqhtApp is custodial. When you create or restore a wallet, your recovery phrase is sent to that server, which derives your node's keys from it. Whoever operates the server can see your node's activity and can spend its funds.

1. Who is responsible

LiqhtApp is a free, open-source app. Each build is configured to connect to one ember server, which runs your Lightning node. The operator of that server holds your node's keys and the information described in section 5, and is responsible for it.

The app sends nothing anywhere else on its own: no analytics, telemetry or advertising services.

2. Data we collect

None. To be specific, LiqhtApp does not collect, transmit or store:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers or any account identifiers — the app has no sign-up
  • Analytics, usage statistics, event tracking or performance telemetry
  • Crash reports or diagnostic logs
  • Advertising identifiers; the app contains no ads and no advertising SDKs
  • Contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone or location data — none of these permissions are requested

The app declares exactly one Android permission: INTERNET, needed to reach the ember server and your node.

3. What is stored on your device

The app keeps the following on your phone:

  • Your recovery phrase: the 24-word BIP39 seed that controls your funds. A copy is also sent to the ember server once, when you create or restore the wallet (see section 5)
  • Device certificate: the credential the ember server issues to this phone when your node is registered or recovered, used to authenticate every later connection
  • Preferences: your display unit and network, which are not sensitive and are stored in plain app storage

How it is protected

  • Encrypted with AES-GCM using a key generated inside the Android Keystore, hardware-backed by StrongBox where your device provides a secure element
  • The key cannot be used while your device is locked, and its material never leaves secure hardware
  • The app is excluded from Android cloud and device backups (allowBackup=false), so your seed is never copied off the phone by the system
  • Screens that display your recovery phrase block screenshots and screen recording
  • Copying your seed or private key marks the clipboard entry sensitive and clears it automatically after 60 seconds
  • Release builds emit no logs

Uninstalling LiqhtApp, or using Delete wallet data in Settings, erases all of this from the device. Neither action affects your node or what the ember server holds, and neither is recoverable without your recovery phrase — keep it written down offline.

5. What the ember server can see and do

This is the most important thing to understand about using LiqhtApp, so it is stated plainly.

When you create or restore a wallet, the app sends your recovery phrase to the ember server over an encrypted, mutually authenticated connection. The server derives your node's signing keys from it and runs your Core Lightning node on its own infrastructure. Its operator can therefore:

  • Spend your node's funds. The node's keys exist on the server, not only on your phone. LiqhtApp is custodial.
  • See your node's identity and the fact that it is online
  • See your channels, balances, asset holdings and the payments your node sends, receives and forwards
  • See the IP address your phone connects from

Only use LiqhtApp with a server operator you trust, and only with amounts you are prepared to entrust to them. If you are not comfortable with that, this app is not the right tool for you.

6. Liquid and Lightning are public networks

Independently of this app: Liquid transactions are recorded permanently on a public blockchain (amounts and asset types are confidential, but transactions and addresses are not). Lightning payments are more private, but routing nodes necessarily learn about the payments they forward. LiqhtApp cannot change either of these properties, and no privacy policy can.

7. Children

LiqhtApp is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 18.

8. Your rights

Privacy regulations such as the GDPR and CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export and delete personal data held about you, and to object to its processing.

The app itself sends no personal data to anyone other than the ember server. Requests about what that server holds go to its operator. Data on your own device is under your sole control and can be erased at any time from Settings or by uninstalling the app.

9. Security disclosure

LiqhtApp handles real funds and has not undergone a third-party security audit. If you find a vulnerability, please report it privately through the repository rather than opening a public issue, so it can be fixed before it is disclosed.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published at this address with a new effective date. Because the app's source is public, changes to what it actually does are visible in the commit history as well.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or the app can be raised at github.com/liquid-lightning/light-app/issues.
